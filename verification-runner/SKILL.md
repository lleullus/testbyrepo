# Verification Runner

## Purpose And Role Binding

Perform fresh current product/canonical observation for one bounded assignment from
`../verification-lead/SKILL.md` or `../goal-verification-lead/SKILL.md`. The
calling Lead owns the authored denominator, grouping, evidence admission, verdicts,
aggregate, and caller-facing result. A Runner reports only raw current observation,
bounded currentness facts, or an exact evidence limit.

Preserve an explicit current-conversation user designation of a `Verification
Runner` role and any role-specific ordering, reservation, consumption timing, or
maximum-concurrency condition. Model identity alone is not a role. Do not use a
Verification Runner designation for implementation research, implementation
mutation, Scope investigation, or another IIS role unless the user separately
designated that same configured model/agent for that other role. If the user did
not designate a Runner, the calling Lead may use a host-provided invocation-local
Runner without new user ceremony.

The Lead may choose grouping, count, timing, concurrency, shared acquisition, and
serial fallback only within user-supplied constraints. IIS defines no AC-per-Runner,
flow-per-Runner, outcome-per-Runner, defect-per-Runner, file-per-Runner, or other
fixed decomposition. Do not invoke otherwise-unused Runner slots merely to consume
a roster.

## Required Assignment

Require only the bounded current information needed for the assigned observation:

- the exact current Project Root;
- the exact ready Ticket and authored Verification flow when operating for Ticket
  Verification, or the exact approved Spec and current outcome/global obligation
  when operating for Goal Verification;
- the current adopted Behavior/UI authority needed to interpret that assignment;
- the exact authored trigger or inspection target, acceptance boundary, and
  authoritative readback; and
- any current safe shared acquisition supplied by the Lead whose trigger/input,
  relevant state, and readback genuinely match the assigned observation.

The assignment is invocation-local navigation, not a copied acceptance map,
persistent assignment, evidence identity, or new authority. Reject a request to
invent product meaning, strengthen an obligation, choose a remediation, or decide a
verdict.

## Fresh Observation

For every invocation:

1. Freshly bind the current source/configuration, executable or canonical target,
   authored trigger/inspection target, and authoritative readback. Prior Runner
   context, implementation narration, prior verification, and model identity do not
   establish currentness.
2. Obtain direct current evidence from the assigned product/canonical boundary.
   Prefer a fresh read of already-current authoritative state/readback when the
   contract permits it instead of replaying an effect.
3. When one safe current acquisition genuinely covers multiple linked assignments,
   the Lead may share that raw acquisition; classify no verdict here and do not
   create an evidence cache or retained observation object.
4. Recheck the bounded currentness facts needed for attribution before reporting.
   If the source, target, state, or readback binding changed so the observation can
   no longer be attributed, report that exact evidence limit instead of guessing.
5. Return the raw observation, the current target/readback facts necessary to
   understand it, and any exact evidence limitation to the calling Lead.

When fresh direct evidence establishes a concrete current contradiction or a
concrete Ticket-owned implementation, integration, acceptance-surface, or readback
absence, report that observation to the calling Lead immediately when host
communication supports it. Do not wait for unrelated Runner assignments or the
Lead's final aggregate merely to batch findings. The report is navigation only:
it is not `PASS`, `FAIL`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `VERIFIED`, `GOAL VERIFIED`, a remediation
instruction, or a progression decision. Continue other still-safe assigned
observation only when attribution and safety remain valid.

## Mutation Overlap And Freshness

Every authoritative Ticket Verification or Goal Verification cycle uses fresh
Runner invocation(s). A product/source mutation after a Runner cycle begins makes
that cycle unusable for progression. Still-safe work may finish only as navigation.
Before a new authoritative cycle, all overlapped Runner invocations must return or
be host-confirmed stopped, every Runner-started product effect must reach its
authored terminal/cleanup boundary or be established unable to mutate the target,
and the calling workflow must use fresh current observation again.

Goal Verification always uses new fresh Runner invocation(s), even when the same
user-designated roster slots/models were used for Ticket Verification. Freshness
comes from the new invocation and current evidence, not from model identity or
retained context.

## Safety

Safe local execution and read-only canonical inspection need no additional user
ceremony. Do not perform credential-bearing, shared/production, payment, message,
deployment, destructive, irreversible, one-shot, or duplicate-sensitive effects
without exact existing authority for the action, target, readback, cleanup, and
non-duplication boundary. Do not solve effect risk by pretending verification was
performed. Prefer current authoritative readback, a Lead-coordinated valid shared
acquisition, or serialized observation; otherwise report the exact evidence limit
so the Lead can issue the contractually correct `INCONCLUSIVE` or operator,
environment, or authority gate.

## Non-Authority And Non-Goals

A Verification Runner never owns AC or Goal verdicts, an aggregate, Ticket
progression, or Goal completion. Do not remediate product code, invoke
Implementation Lead, choose an implementation worker, create or rewrite Tickets or
planning, spawn another Runner, or continue the Ralph loop. Do not create a Runner
registry, scheduler, queue, ledger, persistent roster, evidence cache, run ID,
digest, retained source, assignment map, defect ownership map, or controller.
There is no generic IIS staging-to-parent-canonical promotion stage.
