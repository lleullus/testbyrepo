# Scope Planner dispatch projection

This projection selects the invocation inputs for the Planner. The Planner's method and return contract remain in `scope-plan/SKILL.md` and `references/plan.md`.

## Required

- Exact Project Root.
- Every Scope-bound Thesis original with exact path/revision and actual-byte SHA-256.
- Exact ready Scope path and actual-byte SHA-256.
- Applicable Transition Authority exact paths/digests, or `None` when absent.
- Supplied Repository Investigation and load-bearing evidence locators with applicability conditions, or `None supplied`.
- Exact predecessor Verify/Probe finding, primary evidence, reproducer/limit and settled-effect state when corrective planning applies.
- Current request, permitted planning/scratch authority, stop/no-reentry limits and selected mode/model/effort.
- Exact Plan destination.
- Current repository/target identity needed for code grounding.

## Optional

- Existing related Plan/history anchors when they can answer a bounded current premise.
- Current environment limits that may require conditional first work.

## Forbidden framing

- Do not prescribe the technical solution, cause or change structure as the expected answer.
- Do not supply a fixed failure roster, finding count or Cartesian risk matrix.
- Do not ask the Planner to implement, verify, mutate Scope/Thesis or obtain a review verdict.
- Do not convert a predecessor finding into proof about the current target.

## Assignment pattern

```text
Prepare the grounded executable method for this exact ready Scope from the bound product meaning and current implementation, including only the material implementation-grounded failure frontier and discriminating checks needed by the Scope.
```

## Independence guard

Supplied findings and earlier frontier items are navigation evidence, not presumed complete or current. Ground the method in direct current sources and preserve unresolved premises at their actual strength.

## Result

Write the exact Plan and return the Planner result required by the owning contract: grounding classifications, failure frontier and discriminators, executable disposition/prerequisites, conditional start bundles, evidence locators, working-tree assumptions and limits. Do not issue `ADMIT` or another role's verdict.
