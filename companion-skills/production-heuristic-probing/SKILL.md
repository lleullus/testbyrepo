# Production Heuristic Probing

## Caller contract

For completion lanes supply exact Project Root, admitted fixed Scope and all bound Thesis/approved Transition refs; stable source/artifact/runtime/mechanism binding; lane ID and assigned actual surfaces, budget/minimum attempts; current action/stop authority, protected surfaces, mutable namespaces and settlement/cleanup owner; selected model/effort; actual independent invocation/result destination. Read `../../../iis-workflow/references/assurance.md` and `dispatch/probe.md` from the same source snapshot before dispatch.

Standalone investigation uses the exact requested boundary and target. Scope/Thesis may be None only when no canonical Scope is applicable; it returns investigation evidence only, never completion eligibility. No prior whole-Scope verdict or implementation Plan is a prerequisite. Do not invent missing product promises.

Before hypothesis recording provide originals/target/surface/safety/budget, not implementation cause/success narrative, Plan candidate findings, full Baseline rationale or peer results. After the lane records concise testable hypotheses, disclose needed execution recipes/raw predecessor evidence. Original product authority and safety facts are never hidden. Preserve actual dispatch/disclosure evidence. Shared workspace reading restrictions are procedural unless the host actually enforces them; do not claim statistical independence from model diversity.

### Evidence and effect boundary — copy into each assignment

- Surrogate readback, seeded success or canned output cannot establish a real boundary it replaces.
- Stable source/config/authority, shared gate mechanisms and protected production data are not Probe mutation surfaces.
- Execute only exact authorized disposable/controlled effects with attributable initial state, readback, settlement and cleanup. Otherwise return the precise observation/authority limit.
- Missing evidence is not no-finding. Services/providers and non-idempotent uncertainty require their actual effect owner, not blind replay.

## Purpose and ownership

Find a reachable minimal counterexample to an existing promise at this exact target. Challenge actual routes, state/identity/ordering, alternate writers/readers, weak oracle and false-success paths. A counterexample may falsify implementation, a Plan method, an approved Transition invariant, or a Thesis-bound factual premise/adopted construction decision. Search is not residual-only: a concrete evidence gap may be investigated directly. Do not re-adjudicate every Acceptance, approve the Plan, adopt replacement product policy/architecture or issue a whole-Scope success verdict. No fixed four-role roster, consensus, extra final judge or hidden fallback.

A normal behavioral comparison may use a targeted attacker and an open-frontier scout, within explicit user model/effort/budget policy. Scout starts from originals and actual target, not a Planner candidate list. Add lanes only for concrete distinct boundaries; names do not establish diverse coverage. Caller predeclares required lanes/surfaces. Every started optional lane/finding/effect remains in fan-in.

## Bounded investigation

1. Read original purpose, accepted failure/recovery/preservation and actual target. Trace real entry → deciding owner/state/effect → authoritative result.
2. Record short testable hypotheses before explanation disclosure. They are test plans, not private reasoning transcripts or approval reports.
3. Choose the smallest discriminating trigger: one input/header/state bit/ordering change or equivalent minimal condition. Prefer actual boundary transitions over repeating a known happy path.
4. Execute within authority, keeping identity/state intact through required terminal/recovery and next operation. Distinguish transport success from application readiness, helper results from actual entry and cleaned state from original state.
5. For a concrete finding inspect one bounded same-assumption sibling family sharing actual parser/owner/token/helper. Do not scan by name similarity or hunt remote possibilities after the deciding frontier is resolved.
6. Disposition every material candidate as evidence-backed dismissal, attributable counterexample or precise unobservable boundary. A file read, unexecuted branch, exhausted budget or process exit alone is not no-finding.

Isolate queue/cache/DB/tenant/browser/session/cleanup resources before concurrent actions. Different worktrees/processes alone do not isolate effects. Serialize a shared mutable boundary when isolation is unavailable. Promptly share urgent safety/containment facts even during narrative embargo.

## Findings, reproducer and settlement

A finding records existing Scope/Thesis anchor (or standalone requested promise), reachable trigger/initial identity, actual wrong readback, raw evidence, mechanism/target attribution, examined sibling boundary and narrow next owner. Preserve exact runnable command/script bytes or durable executor-owned fixed locator, prerequisites and cleanup. An expiring /tmp path or prose label is not usable correction evidence. State regression promotion guidance without editing the stable project harness.

Classify MATERIAL, OUT_OF_SCOPE or UNKNOWN against existing promises. Out-of-scope defects are reported separately unless they invalidate a current load-bearing identity/premise. Ambiguous materiality is an evidence gap, not a reason to silently dismiss or repair. A finding label without concrete trace does not authorize correction.

For each action preserve invocation, surface/hypothesis, initial state, trigger, authoritative readback/raw evidence, before/after target/runtime identity and mutable effect paths. Record explicit no effects when read-only. Started effects require authoritative settlement/cleanup; omitted settlement is never inferred successful.

## Result and termination

Return PRODUCTION HEURISTIC PROBE RESULT with exact originals/target/lane/invocation; hypothesis and actual actions/evidence; finding dispositions and reproducer/sibling handoff; effect/currentness/cleanup; and both axes:

- Search: COUNTEREXAMPLE_FOUND, NO_COUNTEREXAMPLE_WITHIN_BUDGET or UNOBSERVABLE.
- Completion: COMPLETE, PARTIAL or BLOCKED.

COMPLETE means the bounded investigation and effects settled; it may contain a counterexample. NO_COUNTEREXAMPLE_WITHIN_BUDGET requires actual assigned-surface attempts and resolved material candidates, not universal correctness. Incomplete/missing/unknown never become no-finding. Completion lanes additionally return the common structured result under assurance.md; standalone need not fabricate an assurance binding.

Stop at the predeclared budget/frontier after recording unresolved candidates honestly. Preserve implementation/source identity unchanged. Unexpected target change or urgent containment leaves the interrupted invocation PARTIAL/BLOCKED with attributable evidence and effects; do not resume an old result on new bytes.

## Caller continuation

Main waits for terminal delivery and all started effects before normal corrective mutation, preserves unchanged results, and computes structural closure with direct observations/native gate evidence. No-finding cannot replace direct observation or cancel a material finding. A failed observation, material counterexample, required gap or unknown settlement prevents recording.

Classify a material finding by the earliest decision that must change: Thesis promise/premise/adopted construction decision, Transition invariant, Plan method/oracle, or faithful implementation. Return the finding, usable reproducer, current target and raw evidence to that owner. The Probe does not adopt replacement meaning or architecture. Multiple no-finding lanes never cancel one material finding, and no-finding is bounded search evidence rather than a correctness proof. New target completion evidence is fresh; prior traces guide the predecessor trigger and directly connected siblings but do not carry forward success. Main's status-only completion procedure lives in iis-workflow. The Probe never repairs, records done or becomes a final approver.
