> [!summary] Summary
> If the 'x509: certificate signed by unknown authority' error occurs when creating a Harbor image registry Secret, register SECDS-ROOT.crt (CA certificate) as a ConfigMap in each Member Cluster and mount it to ks-apiserver.

---

## 1. Symptoms

If the CA certificate has not been applied, the following error occurs when creating a Harbor image registry Secret:

> `x509: certificate signed by unknown authority`

---

## 2. Resolution procedure

### Procedure

1. **Create a ConfigMap (register the CA certificate)**

   KubeSphere menu > **Platform** > **Cluster Management** > each **Member Cluster**

   **Configuration** > **Configmaps** > **Create**

   - **Name**: `secds-rootca` (if the Xi'an CA certificate has a different name, use that name)
   - **Project**: `kubesphere-system`

   **Next** > **Add Data** > enter the **key and value**

   - **Key**: `ca.crt`
   - **Value**: Paste the contents of SECDS-ROOT.crt (use the Xi'an CA certificate contents)

   Click **Create**

2. **Mount the ConfigMap to ks-apiserver**

   KubeSphere menu > **Platform** > **Cluster Management** > each **Member Cluster**

   **Application Workloads** > **Workloads** > `ks-apiserver`

   **More** > **Edit Settings**

   **Volumes** > **Mount Configmap or Secret**

   Select the ConfigMap → click **OK**

   After ks-apiserver restarts automatically, the CA certificate is applied.
