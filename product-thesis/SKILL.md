# Product Thesis

Thesis owns what the product promises and why, including the behavior necessary to make that promise true. The current planning owner performs this work directly unless current user authority explicitly selects another permitted execution arrangement. Write in the user's language.

Use [references/exploration.md](references/exploration.md) when performing product-definition or refinement work. It remains this role's semantic procedure, not a separate product-approval owner. On an enforcement-capable host, Product Thesis work additionally uses the executor-owned lifecycle in `tools/lifecycle.py`: the host records original-input frontier review, fixes an exact candidate, records candidate counterexample review, tracks unresolved material work and accepts `CALIBRATED` only after lifecycle closure. A sufficient existing Thesis may be reused, but a current downstream admission cannot manufacture or inherit a closure record.

## Entry and authority

The single IIS entry interprets the current request first. A discussion or status query is not permission to write product authority. An explicit Thesis request ends after its requested product-definition result. Main's current-state reconciliation and Scope authoring may create or amend Thesis when applicable meaning is missing; sufficient existing meaning is reused without a new ceremony.

Current user instructions take precedence over adopted product originals and derived necessity. Current implementation is not automatic product policy, but it can be load-bearing evidence for the current product decision. Distinguish an adopted product obligation, an observed current system fact, an adopted construction decision and an unresolved premise; do not collapse one into another. Within current delegated product-decision authority, Product-Thesis may adopt a technical construction decision when it is needed to make the promised result determinate and deciding evidence is sufficient. That decision authority does not itself grant implementation, deployment or other effect authority. Preserve explicit required outcomes and required means. Candidate or replaceable means are not completion obligations. Separate direct user choices from agent-derived decisions under actual delegation.

## Executor-owned progress and closure

Product Thesis is the one IIS area allowed to use bounded executor-owned work state because premature semantic completion is itself an upstream correctness failure. This state is not product authority, a second specification or a new approval role.

The enforced phases are `WORKING → CANDIDATE_READY → CHALLENGING → CLOSURE_CHECK → CLOSED`. Rework returns to `WORKING`; genuinely unresolved user choice, decisive evidence limits, exhausted budget, cancellation or host failure end in their explicit non-success states. A process exit, an authored `CALIBRATED` string or a model-produced JSON flag never creates closure.

The trusted supervisor, not the model or worker CLI, records actual review invocation start/completion and pins each review to the host-owned current request generation and exact candidate generation. Before closure it requires:
- one original-input frontier/omission review that is not satisfied by the author's own already-complete narrative,
- one counterexample review against the exact current candidate,
- a grounded disposition for every known material frontier item,
- an actual discriminator or exact stopping boundary for decision-bearing depth,
- no Thesis-blocking semantic/binding premise treated as established while unresolved,
- no unresolved material counterexample finding.

Changing the candidate creates a new fixed candidate and invalidates candidate-specific closure. Previous observations may remain evidence when still applicable, but the final candidate receives a fresh bounded counterexample review. The lifecycle records `CALIBRATED`; the Thesis document itself does not carry a success label.

The fixed candidate and review records live in the supervisor-owned artifact store outside the worker's mutable project surface. References are opaque executor-allocated `{snapshot, path}` values, never content-derived identifiers. Strong enforcement is available only through the UID-separated Linux supervisor: workers cannot choose the store, write lifecycle/review records, replace the trusted current request, or mint downstream admission. Without that boundary, Product Thesis work is advisory and cannot issue an enforced `CALIBRATED`.

## Request intents

Interpret the user's product-definition purpose, not a required command phrase. Preserve any optional focus and explicit limits.

- **Start product meaning:** establish or reuse the applicable source and define the complete core promise. An existing applicable Thesis is not ignored merely because the user says to start.
- **Continue the Thesis:** select the next decision-relevant unresolved, contradictory or omitted meaning from the current source and integrate only an actual semantic contract delta. If no such focus remains, reuse the source and stop without manufacturing another revision.
- **Integrate a supplied requirement:** preserve its provenance and obligation strength, reconcile overlap or conflict with existing meaning, and update only affected consequences. Mention does not automatically promote a candidate or future capability into a current required outcome.
- **Assess sufficiency:** reconstruct the current semantic frontier and report settled meaning and exact gaps without adopting meaning or writing a source unless the user separately authorizes refinement.

These intents use the same bounded exploration procedure. They create no additional product authority or downstream effect authority. The executor lifecycle phases above are bounded work-control state for Product Thesis only, not product statuses or a general IIS workflow engine. A user focus guides selection but cannot exclude a connected material distinction whose answer can make that focus false. Technical vocabulary alone does not make a question method-only. Translate an implementation-worded focus into its product-significant outcome, then ask whether changing that technical fact or choice would change success, failure, identity, preservation, recovery, required means or the current Scope result. Route it away from Thesis only when the product decision is already determinate and genuinely equivalent faithful methods remain open.

## Bounded exploration and refinement

Thesis may be developed iteratively. The failure to avoid is losing why meaning changed, what remains unknown and which connected obligations need reassessment, not making more than one revision. Treat a claimed user problem as supplied experience, observed evidence or an untested hypothesis at its actual strength; do not invent a measured shortfall to justify this procedure or a preferred feature.

Start from the current request, exact applicable originals and one decision-relevant focus question. For initial definition the question may cover the complete core promise. Breadth identifies connected meaning areas and unresolved siblings; Depth follows a selected claim, counterexample or causal relation only far enough to change that decision. Neither is a workflow mode, a fixed checklist or an instruction to descend automatically into Scope, Plan or implementation.

Distinguish direct observations, evidence-backed inferences, untested hypotheses, unknowns and counterexamples from adopted product obligations and adopted construction decisions. A plausible failure is not an observed incident; a refuted cause must not become product policy. First decide what the finding could change. Do not classify a fact or choice as method-only merely because it is technical: if the current product decision depends on an actual state owner, runtime, persistence/effect boundary, readback or concrete construction choice, investigate it deeply enough to decide or preserve the exact unresolved premise. An implementation violating clear meaning, an omitted Scope obligation, a genuinely open method problem or an evidence gap goes to its existing owner through Main rather than into an ever-growing Thesis.

Use direct authorized reads when sufficient. `repository-investigation` is an optional evidence capability for material cross-boundary tracing, competing explanations or reusable evidence handoff; use its owning contract when selected, without a mandatory investigation artifact, hidden delegation or new model choice. A supplied `PRODUCT-COMPLETION-BRIEF` may provide selective false-success and missing-meaning questions. It is not required input, a checklist to pass or permission to expand this request.

When meaning must change, adopt it under existing authority, preserve referenced revisions, and inspect only connected semantic consequences and downstream impact. Stop this exploration when the focus question is decided, a decisive evidence/authority limit is reached, further investigation cannot change the decision, or another owner must act. Report the exact unresolved boundary; then obey the current request's continuation or stop rule. Exploration sufficiency is neither product completion nor a verifier verdict.

## Minimum semantic sufficiency

For the current focus decision, derive a bounded **semantic frontier** from the current request, exact applicable originals, the complete core utility loop, explicit required outcomes/means, concrete observations or contradictions, and any meaning distinction that can change the current Scope choice. This is not a fixed list of identity, recovery, persistence or other rows.

Include a distinction when different answers can materially change the user-visible result, its truth or attribution, identity/ownership, failure/recovery/preservation outcome, or the current construction outcome/Acceptance. For each material frontier item, establish one of these ordinary dispositions: the meaning is resolved or an applicable original is reused; a genuinely user-owned choice remains; an evidence/authority limit is preserved without turning it into product policy; the issue belongs to another existing owner; or the item is excluded with a concrete basis because it cannot change the current result. These are reasoning outcomes, not new canonical statuses. Do not enumerate irrelevant lenses merely to prove coverage.

Assess frontier Breadth and decision Depth separately before requesting semantic closure. Deep resolution of one item cannot disposition an unexamined material sibling, and enumerating siblings cannot resolve a decision-bearing item without a discriminator or exact stopping boundary. These are reasoning and closure obligations, not required Thesis sections, checklist rows or a separate evidence ledger.

A lifecycle `CALIBRATED` closure is eligible only when the current focus decision and core utility are clear, success truth/readback meaning is settled, every material frontier item has such a disposition, no unresolved current product choice remains, and a concrete material counterexample cannot still pass while the adopted meaning is false. Downstream Scope/Plan/Implementer must not need to invent product truth, identity, loss tolerance or recovery policy; any Thesis-bound construction decision required for the current request is closed; no decisive current-system premise is being used as though observed when it is not; and remaining unknowns are distinguished between ones that can change product meaning/decision and ones that only limit implementation satisfaction evidence. Evidence gaps, implementation contradictions and non-applicable future details do not become product choices. Respect the current stop boundary. This closure is not implementation completion or runtime satisfaction, and later evidence or a Probe may refute a premise or decision and require an immutable revision.

## Product promise

Establish the concrete shortfall, durable user/operator utility and complete causal loop from intent to actual outcome. A feature list or polished interface cannot replace the result. Define the entire core promise even when current construction will cover only part of it. Do not weaken the whole product to make the first Scope easier.

Explain the necessary behaviors: which input or situation requires judgment, transformation or preservation; what the user actually obtains; and why omission would make the promise impossible or false. Identify plausible false successes. Saving a revised story is not sufficient if generation still uses the original; correct input lineage alone does not prove the generated pictures reflect the revision.

## Behavior and UI policy

Thesis is the canonical product home for relevant identity, ownership, state, transition, allowed/forbidden behavior, interruption, failure, recovery, repetition, ordering, concurrency and preservation rules. Describe only distinctions that affect actual product outcomes. A state table is useful when it clarifies behavior, not a mandatory checklist.

Include rendered states and interactions that materially affect the promise: what users can select, what is preserved, what progress/success/failure means and how results remain attributable. Reuse applicable design assets and existing product vocabulary. Visual prototypes or assets may be referenced without creating a separate approval lifecycle. Technical vocabulary does not determine decision ownership. Product-Thesis may investigate architecture, files, APIs, libraries, state ownership, persistence, runtime, scheduling and authoritative readback as deeply as needed for the current product decision. When current authority and deciding evidence are sufficient, it may also adopt a concrete construction decision required to make the promised result determinate. Record whether a load-bearing statement is an adopted product obligation, observed current fact, adopted construction decision or unresolved premise. Plan owns faithful execution details and remaining open method choices; it does not inherit undecided product truth merely because the question is technical.

For current Scope selection, resolve product choices that materially change which outcome should be built. Before that Scope is ready for execution planning, close every material choice affecting its promised behavior, failure/recovery, UI and acceptance. Future capability details may remain open. An incomplete future section is not a reason to invent its policy or restart the whole Thesis.

## Success and counterexamples

State actual observable success and its authoritative readback. Define truth/causal invariants so identities, persistence, execution and outcomes cannot be substituted by local, cached, mock or agent-reported state when the promised boundary is real. A disposable environment is not fake if it exercises the approved real boundary; a test label does not prove it does.

Challenge the proposed meaning:
- Could all named features exist while utility is absent?
- Could the loop look complete while a necessary behavior is missing?
- Could displayed success belong to the wrong identity, revision or cause?
- Could failure, repetition or late output destroy a result the product promises to preserve?

Resolve only material contradictions. Do not invent adjacent requirements to make review look thorough. Product acceptance meaning belongs here; Scope makes its current application observable. The planning owner maps those obligations to actual evidence and independent attack surfaces; structural closure does not replace product meaning or create another semantic judge.

## Decisions and confirmation

Apply current explicit instructions, adopted originals, decision priorities, delegated authority and inspectable facts before asking. Ask the user only when (1) two or more semantic outcomes genuinely remain, (2) their difference changes the user-visible result, truth/attribution, material failure/recovery/preservation behavior or current Scope choice, and (3) applicable instructions, adopted originals, delegated priorities and available deciding evidence cannot select one. When those sources determine one faithful result, adopt it within authority without claiming direct user approval. A hypothetical future detail, replaceable implementation method or evidence-access problem alone does not require a product-policy question.

No separate Matt shared understanding, Behavior approval phase or Spec serialization is created. Main resolves a material product gap in the affected original section here before fixing the current Scope. Preserve settled meaning and update only its causal consequences. A user's explicit approval gate still applies at its actual IIS boundary.

## Source storage and currentness

Use an exact project-local source under `docs/planning/product-thesis/<meaning-slug>/THESIS-NNN.md`. Preserve referenced revisions unchanged. A changed binding contract receives a new ordinal. A new ordinal is required when product meaning changes, required means change, a Thesis-bound construction decision that actually constrains downstream Scope/Plan changes, or a load-bearing factual premise is refuted such that a faithful downstream plan must change. A behavioral semantic delta still asks whether the set of compliant product behaviors changes and uses the compliant-product test: a reasonable product could satisfy the old required/allowed/forbidden result, truth/identity boundary or material failure/recovery/preservation rule while violating the newly adopted meaning, or vice versa. A new ordinal is not required merely because an evidence locator is added while meaning and binding decisions stay the same, an implementation defect is found while the existing obligation/decision still stands, or wording is cleaned up without changing the range of faithful downstream plans. The ordinary revision/reuse rationale should make clear whether the relevant change is a semantic contract delta, required-means delta, adopted-construction delta or evidence/rationale-only update; these are explanatory distinctions, not new artifact fields or a schema. Reuse one source across Scopes instead of copying it into each Scope. For genuinely independent reusable capability contracts, add sources only when there is a real separate canonical meaning, not by template quota.

The template is a content guide, not a fixed required field count. A small existing-rule correction can reuse the source unchanged. Keep future choices in the relevant source with the condition before which they must be decided. Distinguish unresolved core meaning from future detail: the latter need not block a current Scope that does not depend on it.

An unrooted explicit product-definition request may store its source in one reported temporary location. Before durable Scope binding, preserve the exact adopted bytes at the project-local location. Storage is not approval or authorization to implement.

Scope v2 references every applicable executor-owned fixed original with an opaque snapshot/path reference. The store owns the fixed bytes; the reference is allocated, not calculated from content, and it proves neither product meaning nor approval. A new unrelated revision does not invalidate an older bound contract. A newer user instruction affecting the bound contract requires an explicit affected-source/Scope reassessment. Never overwrite history to make old evidence look current.

Keep material unresolved meaning in the source and deciding evidence locators, revision rationale and affected downstream references in the ordinary result, commit explanation or existing project record as needed. Preserve original evidence attribution and state retrieval limits. Do not create an exploration ledger or append every rejected hypothesis to Thesis. An exact fixed-source reference, section and Thesis ordinal suffice for navigation; no semantic Node graph or alias registry is required.

Apply the reference's currentness distinctions only where they can change this product decision. No Scope binding or deployed runtime is a prerequisite for defining a new product. Missing runtime evidence cannot prove runtime behavior, but it need not prevent a product meaning already determined by the user's requirements.

## Result and boundary

Report the actual source, product promise, material choices and unresolved limitations. For refinement, include the changed meaning, deciding evidence or counterexample, material remaining unknowns and bounded ripple/next-owner result where relevant; no mandatory terminal form is added. For any lifecycle-closed, new-revision or unchanged-source-reuse result, the ordinary result must make the focus decision, selected semantic frontier and its basis, deciding counterexample/discriminator, settled or reused meaning, any material user-owned choice or evidence limit, any load-bearing exclusion basis, and the revision decision recoverable from concise prose. For a `CALIBRATED` lifecycle closure, the concise result must make independently recoverable both the Breadth basis for why no material sibling raised by the inclusion test lacks a grounded disposition and the Depth discriminator or exact stopping boundary for decision-bearing items; these are not required headings or fields. Do not create a second frontier artifact or force headings for a trivial case. `CALIBRATED` is emitted by the lifecycle only after minimum semantic sufficiency work and required host-recorded reviews close; it does not mean every future policy is specified, implementation is correct or the product exists. `USER_INPUT_REQUIRED` identifies the exact unresolved current product choice. Preserve the unresolved source honestly when storage is authorized.

A decisive missing evidence or capability limit is not automatically a user-owned product choice. Preserve that boundary in lifecycle state and ordinary prose; it becomes an explicit waiting/incomplete result rather than a fabricated `CALIBRATED`. Non-decisive unknowns remain explicit without blocking settled meaning.

Product-Thesis may adopt a technical decision required for the current product decision under the authority and evidence rules above. It does not own construction order, implicit Scope selection, implementation, deployment, evidence execution or completion verdicts. A Thesis-only request stores the authorized decision and stops. Adopting a technical construction decision is not downstream execution authority. Return to the same IIS Main, which obeys the user's requested stage boundary. Product source storage does not authorize further stages.
