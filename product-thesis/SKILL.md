# Product Thesis

Thesis owns what the product promises and why, including the behavior necessary to make that promise true. The current planning owner performs this work directly unless current user authority explicitly selects another permitted execution arrangement. Write in the user's language.

## Entry and authority

The single IIS entry interprets the current request first. A discussion or status query is not permission to write product authority. An explicit Thesis request ends after its requested product-definition result. Main's current-state reconciliation and Scope authoring may create or amend Thesis when applicable meaning is missing; sufficient existing meaning is reused without a new ceremony.

Current user instructions take precedence over adopted product originals and derived necessity. Current implementation is evidence, not automatic product policy. Preserve explicit required outcomes and required means. Candidate or replaceable means are not completion obligations. Separate direct user choices from agent-derived decisions under actual delegation.

## Product promise

Establish the concrete shortfall, durable user/operator utility and complete causal loop from intent to actual outcome. A feature list or polished interface cannot replace the result. Define the entire core promise even when current construction will cover only part of it. Do not weaken the whole product to make the first Scope easier.

Explain the necessary behaviors: which input or situation requires judgment, transformation or preservation; what the user actually obtains; and why omission would make the promise impossible or false. Identify plausible false successes. Saving a revised story is not sufficient if generation still uses the original; correct input lineage alone does not prove the generated pictures reflect the revision.

## Behavior and UI policy

Thesis is the canonical product home for relevant identity, ownership, state, transition, allowed/forbidden behavior, interruption, failure, recovery, repetition, ordering, concurrency and preservation rules. Describe only distinctions that affect actual product outcomes. A state table is useful when it clarifies behavior, not a mandatory checklist.

Include rendered states and interactions that materially affect the promise: what users can select, what is preserved, what progress/success/failure means and how results remain attributable. Reuse applicable design assets and existing product vocabulary. Visual prototypes or assets may be referenced without creating a separate approval lifecycle. Technical architecture, files, internal APIs, libraries and scheduling belong to Plan, not Thesis.

For current Scope selection, resolve product choices that materially change which outcome should be built. Before that Scope is ready for execution planning, close every material choice affecting its promised behavior, failure/recovery, UI and acceptance. Future capability details may remain open. An incomplete future section is not a reason to invent its policy or restart the whole Thesis.

## Success and counterexamples

State actual observable success and its authoritative readback. Define truth/causal invariants so identities, persistence, execution and outcomes cannot be substituted by local, cached, mock or agent-reported state when the promised boundary is real. A disposable environment is not fake if it exercises the approved real boundary; a test label does not prove it does.

Challenge the proposed meaning:
- Could all named features exist while utility is absent?
- Could the loop look complete while a necessary behavior is missing?
- Could displayed success belong to the wrong identity, revision or cause?
- Could failure, repetition or late output destroy a result the product promises to preserve?

Resolve only material contradictions. Do not invent adjacent requirements to make review look thorough. Product acceptance meaning belongs here; Scope makes its current application observable, and the verifier owns exact evidence collection and adjudication.

## Decisions and confirmation

Apply current explicit instructions, adopted originals, decision priorities, delegated authority and inspectable facts before asking. When those determine one faithful recommendation, adopt it within authority without claiming direct user approval. When materially different user-owned results remain, explain their consequences and ask only that choice. A hypothetical future detail or replaceable implementation method does not require a question.

No separate Matt shared understanding, Behavior approval phase or Spec serialization is created. Main resolves a material product gap in the affected original section here before fixing the current Scope. Preserve settled meaning and update only its causal consequences. A user's explicit approval gate still applies at its actual IIS boundary.

## Source storage and currentness

Use an exact project-local source under `docs/planning/product-thesis/<meaning-slug>/THESIS-NNN.md`. Preserve referenced revisions unchanged. A changed contract receives a new ordinal. Reuse one source across Scopes instead of copying it into each Scope. For genuinely independent reusable capability contracts, add sources only when there is a real separate canonical meaning, not by template quota.

The template is a content guide, not a fixed required field count. A small existing-rule correction can reuse the source unchanged. Keep future choices in the relevant source with the condition before which they must be decided. Distinguish unresolved core meaning from future detail: the latter need not block a current Scope that does not depend on it.

An unrooted explicit product-definition request may store its source in one reported temporary location. Before durable Scope binding, preserve the exact adopted bytes at the project-local location. Storage is not approval or authorization to implement.

Scope references every applicable exact original and its full UTF-8 SHA-256. Hashes prove byte identity, not product meaning, approval or automatic currentness. A new unrelated revision does not invalidate an older bound contract. A newer user instruction affecting the bound contract requires an explicit affected-source/Scope reassessment; the old hash is not permission to ignore it. Never overwrite history to make old evidence look current.

## Result and boundary

Report the actual source, product promise, material choices and unresolved limitations. `CALIBRATED` means the applicable product meaning is settled and the counterexamples close; it does not mean every future policy is specified or the product exists. `USER_INPUT_REQUIRED` identifies the exact unresolved current product choice. Preserve the unresolved source honestly when storage is authorized.

Do not select construction order, define the current Scope implicitly, choose technical methods, issue verification verdicts or mark delivery complete. Return to the same IIS Main, which obeys the user's requested stage boundary. Product source storage does not authorize further stages.
